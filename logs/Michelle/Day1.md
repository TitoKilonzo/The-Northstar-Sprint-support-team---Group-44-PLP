# Day 1 Reflection — August 13

## What happened

On the first day, I mostly observed as the Team coordinated the setup of the Northstar Support MVP. The focus was on establishing the team’s working process and defining the scope of the MVP. I attended the Google Meet sessions where the team discussed the requirements and agreed to focus on order status and returns/refunds.I followed along with the discussions and acknowledged the Charter once it was finalized.

## What went well

The Team Lead did a great job organizing the team and setting clear expectations through the Charter.
The scope was narrowed down to two categories, which made the MVP more realistic and achievable.
I was able to understand the team’s communication style and workflow, even though I didn’t contribute much technically.



## What was harder than expected

Balancing listening and learning with the expectation to participate was harder than I anticipated


## Self-assessment

On Day 1, my role was more passive than I would have liked. I acknowledged the Charter and participated in discussions, but I didn’t contribute much to the actual setup. I realize that I need to be more proactive in finding tasks I can take ownership of, even if they are small. Going forward, I want to step up and add more technical contributions while continuing to support the team’s process.
