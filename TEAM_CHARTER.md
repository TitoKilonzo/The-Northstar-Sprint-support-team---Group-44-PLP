# Team Charter - Northstar Support MVP

## 1. Purpose

This Charter defines how the team will communicate, divide work, manage deadlines, resolve disagreements, and maintain an auditable record of collaboration during the evaluation sprint.

The team will work toward a Support Deflection MVP that reduces manual support handling for at least two of Northstar Retail Co.'s three identified support categories:
- Order status
- Returns/refunds
- Stock availability

## 2. Communication (WhatsApp Group & Google Meet)

- The team will use the agreed group communication channel for day-to-day coordination.
- The GitHub repository and Project Board will be used to record project work, task status, decisions, and relevant project information.
- Important project messages should be acknowledged within the team's agreed working period where reasonably possible.
- Members who become unavailable should communicate this to the team as soon as reasonably possible.
- Team meetings will focus on decisions, blockers, task allocation, review, and collaboration.

## 3. Availability

- Each member will communicate their realistic availability and expected working hours.
- Members should communicate significant changes in availability that could affect assigned work.
- Availability will be considered when assigning tasks and deadlines.

## 4. Task Ownership

- Every Project Board task must have one clearly identified owner.
- The owner is responsible for progressing the task, keeping its status updated, and satisfying its Definition of Done.
- Other members may collaborate or review the work, but the assigned owner remains accountable for the task.

## 5. Task Size — Anti-Black-Box Rule

- No Project Board task should require more than 4 hours of work.
- If a task is expected to exceed 4 hours, it must be split into smaller tasks.
- Each task must represent a clear, manageable piece of work.
- Each task must have a single, checkable Definition of Done.

## 6. Priorities

The team will use the following priority levels:

- **P0:** Critical or blocking work requiring immediate attention.
- **P1:** High-priority work required for the MVP or submission.
- **P2:** Lower-priority or non-blocking work that can be completed after higher-priority work.

## 7. Definition of Done

Every Project Board task must have a clear Definition of Done.

A task may only be moved to Done when its Definition of Done has been satisfied and, where appropriate, reviewed by another team member.

## 8. Board Status Updates

- Members will update their task status on the same day that the corresponding work takes place.
- Members should move tasks through the agreed workflow as work progresses:
  - To Do
  - In Progress
  - Review
  - Done
- Work should not be accumulated and entered into the board retrospectively at the end of the week.

## 9. Deadlines and Delays

- Members are responsible for completing assigned tasks within agreed timeframes.
- A member who anticipates a delay should inform the team as soon as possible.
- The team will assess the blocker and provide support, adjust the task, or reassign work where necessary.
- Changes to deadlines or task ownership should be reflected on the Project Board.

## 10. Inactivity and Escalation

If a member has zero visible project activity for two or more consecutive days:

1. The team will contact the member to identify any blocker or availability issue.
2. The member will provide an updated status or recovery plan where appropriate.
3. If the member remains unavailable and their task is blocking delivery, the team may reassign the task.
4. The change and reason should be documented on the Project Board.

## 11. Conflict Resolution

- Team members will first discuss disagreements respectfully with the people directly involved.
- The team will refer to the assignment requirements, project objectives, and available evidence when evaluating alternatives.
- If the disagreement cannot be resolved directly, the team will discuss it collectively.
- Decisions that materially affect the project should be documented.

## 12. Collaboration and Accountability

- Every member is expected to make meaningful contributions to the project.
- Contributions should remain traceable through appropriate project records, including board activity, commits, or tracked document edits.
- Members should not claim credit for work they did not perform.
- Team members should support one another when a genuine blocker prevents progress.

## 13. Commit and Edit Convention
Where commits or tracked edits are required, the team will use:

<type>: <what changed> - <why it matters>

Examples:
feat: add order lookup - retrieves customer order information
test: validate return scenarios - checks eligible and ineligible cases
docs: add setup instructions - helps teammates run the project locally
Vague messages such as `wip` or `updates` will not be used.

## 14. Peer Reliability Confidentiality

Peer Reliability Index responses are confidential.

Members will not pressure teammates to disclose individual ratings or share individual responses with other members.

Only permitted aggregate patterns may be discussed or released.

## 15. Charter Changes

If a working rule becomes unclear or impractical, the team may discuss and amend the Charter by agreement.

Material changes will be documented so that the team's working process remains clear and auditable.
